# ✨ Hola! Ixel here (pronounced "ee-shell") ✨ 

I’m an environmental data scientist with a background in ecology and evolution, focused on using data to understand and manage complex ecological systems. I’m particularly interested in applying data to support biodiversity research, conservation, and environmental decision-making to support a more sustainable future. I enjoy collaborating across disciplines to develop creative, data-driven solutions to environmental challenges, and I’m committed to advancing inclusivity and accessibility in data science. My work combines statistical analysis, geospatial data, and reproducible workflows.

### 🌿 Current Work
- 🌲 Developing an R package to provide cloud-based access to 250GB of wildfire resilience geospatial data  
- 🗺️ Supporting spatial analysis and risk assessment for western wildfire-prone ecosystems  
- 🤝 Collaborating with NCEAS to increase access to high-resolution environmental datasets  

### 🧪 Research & Data Interests
